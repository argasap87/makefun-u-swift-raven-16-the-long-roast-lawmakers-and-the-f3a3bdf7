## The roast

> Senator Whitaker grilled a tech CEO on how to regulate AI while his aide, three feet away, whispered, "Sir, the Print-to-PDF button isn't a metaphor — stop printing the email, scanning it, and faxing it to your own iPhone."

The hook: the hearing is the setup — a senator demanding answers on how to regulate AI — and the aide's whisper is the undercut. The lawmaker dodges one button by building a Rube Goldberg machine: print the email, scan the paper, fax it to his own iPhone. The punch lands on the last few words, and the target is the power that writes the rules, not a random bystander.
